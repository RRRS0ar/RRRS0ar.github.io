Customise

Four choices: the club, the name, the number and the size. The picture shows how the print sits on the back before you send anything.

Drawing of the print we make: one colour, 30 cm tall, centred between the shoulder blades, 4 cm under the collar. Not a photo of a real shirt.

How it works

  1. 1Pick the club. The club sets the shirt colour and the badge on the chest.
  2. 2Write the name. Twelve letters is the limit. Capitals stay as you type them.
  3. 3Choose a number. Anything from 1 to 99. Two digits print narrower than one.
  4. 4Check the size, then send. The size table is on the Shop page. We confirm the stock before you pay.
