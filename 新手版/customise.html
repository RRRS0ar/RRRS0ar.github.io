Customise

You choose four things: the club, the name, the number and the size. The picture shows how the print looks on the back.

Drawing of the print, not a photo of a real shirt. One colour, 30 cm tall, in the middle of the back.

How it works

  1. Pick the club.
  2. Write the name. Twelve letters is the most.
  3. Choose a number from 1 to 99.
  4. Check your size in the size guide, then send the order.

Your shirt

The button opens your e-mail program with the order ready. A person in the shop reads it and writes back before you pay.

What it costs

  • Shirt: 69 euros
  • Name and number: 15 euros
  • Postage in Finland: 4.90 euros
  • Total: 88.90 euros

The price is the same for every club and every name. There is no payment on this page.
