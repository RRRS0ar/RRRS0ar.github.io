Size guide

Put a shirt that fits you flat on a table. Measure it from armpit to armpit and double the number. That is your chest size. Compare it with the table.

Size Chest in cm Length in cm
S 88 - 92 69
M 96 - 100 71
L 104 - 108 73
XL 112 - 116 78

Between two sizes? Take the bigger one if you want to wear the shirt over a hoodie. Take the smaller one if you wear it alone. A printed shirt can only go back if the print is wrong.
