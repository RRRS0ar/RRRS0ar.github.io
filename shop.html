Size guide

Measure a shirt that already fits you, laid flat. Armpit to armpit, then double it. That number is your chest size.

Shirt sizes in centimetres. Chest and length are measured on a flat shirt, chest is the full round.
Size Chest (cm) Length (cm) Fits height
S 88–92 69 165–172 cm
M 96–100 71 172–178 cm
L 104–108 73 178–184 cm
XL 112–116 78 184–190 cm
2XL 120–124 80 190–196 cm

Between two sizes? Take the larger one if you want to wear the shirt over a hoodie, the smaller one if you wear it alone. An unprinted shirt can go back within 30 days. A printed shirt can only go back if the print is wrong, so measure first.
