Four reasons to buy here

  • The chest size is in centimetres. Measure a shirt you already own, flat, from armpit to armpit. Double it and compare with the size table on the Shop page. No guessing between M and L.
  • Stock is visible before the bag. If the card says 3, there are 3 on the shelf. When they are gone the badge turns to sold out, and you can ask to be told when it comes back.
  • The printing happens in this shop. Name and number are pressed in Helsinki in two to three working days. If you spell the name wrong, you can still fix it before we print.
  • One price for every club. A current shirt is 69 euros and the print is 15. Long names cost the same as short ones, and nothing is added at the last step.

What you can check before you pay

  • Stock

    The number on each card is the shelf count we do every morning. No countdown and no "only one left" that stays there for a week.

  • Delivery

    Two to five days in Finland, five to nine days in the rest of the EU. Postage is 4.90 and free over 80 euros. We post on Tuesday and Thursday.

  • The shirts are real

    We buy from the club shop or the club's official supplier. A replica from an older season is named as a replica on the card, so nothing is sold as something it is not.
